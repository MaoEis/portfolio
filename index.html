About me

I started my journey studying social work, drawn by my love for psychology and philosophy. After a few years of traveling and working as a social worker, I learned a lot about myself and others, but realised my passion was in creativity and design.

In my Digital Experience Design studies, I’ve gained skills in tools like Adobe Suite and Figma, along with experience in design thinking, branding, and development.

I thrive on learning new tools, exploring innovative ideas, and finding simple, effective solutions. Collaboration is where I shine—working together to exchange thoughts and analyse challenges is what drives me forward.
